About

Jimit Shah —
Founder.

Independent equity analyst. Mumbai-based. Two years in Indian capital markets with a focus on mid-small cap listed companies.

Avalon Investments is an independent equity research platform focused primarily on Indian listed equities, with an emphasis on mid-small cap companies where institutional sell-side coverage is thin and information edge remains meaningful.

This platform was built on a simple set of principles: rigorous, data-anchored research on Indian companies should not require access to a Bloomberg terminal or a sell-side subscription. Every report published under the Avalon banner follows a consistent methodology — primary source verification, no unverified figures, and analysis that reflects reality as the data shows it.

Prior to founding Avalon, I worked as an Equity research analyst at a CAT-III Alternative Investment Fund, contributing to live investment decisions including a notable position in Timex Group India. That experience forms the analytical foundation that underpins the research process that I have today.

My coverage spans six sectors: Power & T&D, Defence & Aerospace, Pharma & CDMO, Specialty Chemicals, Banks & NBFCs, and Capital Goods & Engineering. Within each sector, the focus is on companies at an inflection point — order book ramp, margin recovery, or a structural shift in competitive positioning.

My research methodology combines bottom-up fundamental analysis with sector-level value chain mapping, DCF modeling, and peer comparison frameworks. Beyond fundamental research, I also traded actively across equity and commodity markets for about 4 years, bringing a practitioner's perspective to both positioning and risk.
